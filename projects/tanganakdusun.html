Overview

TanganakDusun is my final-year project: an interactive 360-degree virtual reality learning platform.

Purpose

The platform was developed to support the preservation of indigenous language and culture through education.

My contribution

I developed the educational platform and designed 360-degree virtual environments, quizzes and multimedia modules.

Process and tools

I used Claude AI to help architect the website layout and debug front-end components. I used ChatGPT to generate creative media, assets and multimedia content for the platform.

Documented outcome

The project brought together 360-degree virtual environments, quizzes and multimedia modules in a VR-based educational platform.
